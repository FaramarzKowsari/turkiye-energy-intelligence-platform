# Public-Sector and Strategic Use Cases

EnerjiNabız AI is structured as reusable research software rather than an official operator product. With validated institutional data and governance, its components can support:

## Demand-pressure monitoring

Compare current consumption with seasonal expectations, identify unusual ramps and prepare daily operational briefs.

## Renewable integration analysis

Measure changes in wind, solar and hydro contribution, quantify forecast errors and study periods of high renewable penetration.

## Public-building energy planning

Combine municipal building consumption with market and weather data to identify avoidable peak demand and evaluate load-shifting scenarios.

## Electric-vehicle charging studies

Estimate low-pressure charging windows and test the system impact of alternative charging schedules.

## Industrial decision support

Provide factories and organized industrial zones with consumption forecasts, anomaly alerts and cost-aware scheduling indicators.

## Research and education

Offer a reproducible Turkish energy-data engineering reference for universities, technical programs and public innovation labs.

No public-sector deployment should rely on synthetic data or unvalidated derived indicators. Institutional pilots require data-sharing terms, domain review and operational acceptance criteria.
