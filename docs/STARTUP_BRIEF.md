# Startup Brief

## Product thesis

Organizations do not pay for raw public energy data; they pay for cleaned history, dependable forecasts, explainable alerts and measurable operational savings.

## Initial customer profiles

- Industrial facilities and organized industrial zones
- Commercial buildings and hospital campuses
- Renewable-energy portfolio operators
- Energy consultants and auditors
- Electric-vehicle charging operators
- Municipal energy-management units

## Minimum sellable product

1. Secure customer data connector
2. Consumption and price forecast
3. Peak and anomaly alerts
4. Load-shifting scenario calculator
5. Automated weekly management report
6. Export/API integration with existing BI systems

## Differentiation

- Turkey-specific market connectors
- Provider-independent architecture
- Data-quality scoring before forecasting
- Open-core trust and reproducibility
- BI-ready outputs instead of a closed dashboard only

## Evidence required for a pilot

- Forecast improvement over a seasonal-naive benchmark
- Alert precision and false-positive rate
- Estimated and realized cost reduction
- Data completeness and latency SLA
- Security and access-control review

## Non-claims

The open-source repository is a technical foundation, not proof of government endorsement, regulatory approval, guaranteed savings or eligibility for residence or citizenship.
